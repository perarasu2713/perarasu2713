A real-time driver monitoring application designed to identify signs of fatigue through facial landmark analysis.

- Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) for eye closure and blink detection.

- Voice warnings and audio alerts.
- Flask dashboard with live camera monitoring and detection statistics.
- SQLite-based session history and CSV monitoring records.
- GPS tracking and Telegram emergency notifications.
- Emergency contact management and Gmail SMTP fallback.

**Technology Stack**

`Python` `OpenCV` `MediaPipe` `Flask` `SQLite` `JavaScript` `Telegram Bot API` `SMTP`
